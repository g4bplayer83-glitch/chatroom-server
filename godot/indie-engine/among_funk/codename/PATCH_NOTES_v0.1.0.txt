STYLE REWORK
- Custom Options, Freeplay, Story Mode, Pause and Patch Notes now match the original Among Funk menu style more closely.

FREEPLAY / STORY
- Freeplay launches the real song charts.

GAMEPLAY

- Change Controls opens Codename Engine's controls over the paused song.
- P toggles BotPlay during gameplay.
